# BCCE 2026 — What chemistry faculty believe about doctoral education

Slide deck for the BCCE 2026 talk, generated from the paper draft
*"What chemistry faculty believe about doctoral education: a phenomenographic
and thematic characterization"* (Zoe Rahimi Pirkoohi & Jordan Harshman,
University of Iowa).

## Design (v2 — restrained academic)
- **Look:** left-aligned, whitespace-forward, no cards / pills / kicker chips /
  oversized quote marks. Built to read as hand-made, not template-generated.
- **Palette:** mostly black-and-white, color used sparingly — BCCE flamingo
  **#F5628C** (recurring emphasis) + University of Iowa gold **#FFCD00** (brand
  moments) + near-black text. One bold black slide ("The Finding") for rhythm.
- **Type:** Constantia (display/serif) + Corbel (labels/sans) — distinctive but
  ships with Office, so it opens correctly on any conference PC.
- **Branding:** light touch — Iowa wordmark + gold rule on the title, quiet footer.

## Files
- **`BCCE2026_faculty_beliefs.pptx`** — the editable deck (16 slides, 16:9).
- **`BCCE2026_preview.pdf`** — quick-look preview. *Note:* rendered with
  substitute fonts (Liberation Serif/Sans), so exact letterforms differ slightly
  from the real deck; use the `.pptx` for the final look.
- **`build_v2.py`** — regenerates the current `.pptx` (`python-pptx`).
- **`make_assets.py`** — rebuilds the figure assets in the new palette
  (desaturated speech-bubble opener + monochrome/flamingo boundary map).
- **`pptx_render.py`** — renders the `.pptx` to PNGs for QA.
- **`build_deck.py`** — the earlier v1 generator (multicolor theme palette), kept
  for reference.
- **`assets/`** — figures. v2 uses `speech_bubbles_gray.png` and
  `boundary_map_v2.png`; `image1.png` (source) is regenerated by `make_assets.py`.

## Deck outline (~15 min, minimal-text)
1. Title
2. The enterprise & its strains
3. The gap — teaching a class vs. advising a PhD
4. Research question
5. Why beliefs matter — Teacher-Centered Systemic Reform
6. What counts as a belief — the 4-part decision rule
7. Methods — 20 faculty, interviews + card sort, 990 → 15 → 4
8. The sample (N = 20)
9. **The finding — agree on propositions, divide on boundaries** (black slide)
10–13. The four themes (representative quote + the axis of division)
14. The full outcome space (recreated boundary map)
15. What this adds
16. Implications + thank you
